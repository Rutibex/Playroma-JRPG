# Community Code of Conduct

Playroma jRPG welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and specific. Focus
criticism on code, behavior, evidence, and compatibility outcomes rather than
on people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption,
and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close disruptive discussions,
or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its
community. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be
reviewed with discretion and without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
