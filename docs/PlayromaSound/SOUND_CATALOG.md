## Continuous Variation Controls

Intensity, duration, apparent size, brightness, roughness, tonal/noise balance, organic/synthetic balance, complexity, movement, distance, damage/corruption, and family-specific controls such as debris, viscosity, equipment noise, instability, population, urgency, engine load, alienness, dread, elasticity, force, modulation, spread, event count, rhythm amount, and repetition.

Equal descriptions and seeds remain deterministic. Variation mode, variation index, and family seed can create stable siblings, subtle identity-preserving alternatives, mutations, crossbreeds, retro-constrained versions, damaged versions, cinematic versions, or deliberately chaotic outliers.
