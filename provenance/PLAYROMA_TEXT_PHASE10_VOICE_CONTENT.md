# Phase 10 Voice Content Provenance

The built-in dialogue voice profiles, vocabulary samples, framing phrases, and Phase 10 recipe lines were authored as original Playroma demonstration content for this implementation.

They are fictional game-writing archetypes and are intentionally not presented as simulations of real-world ethnic, national, regional, disability, class, or other protected/social identities. Project authors may create their own profiles using the declarative `.playvoice.json` format.

No external dialogue corpus is bundled by Phase 10. User-imported corpora remain governed by the Phase 5 source/license metadata system.
