# Phase 5 corpus provenance

The small demonstration corpora in `BuiltInPhase5Recipes.cs` and `PlayromaTextAssets/Phase5/example-tavern.playcorpus.json` were written specifically for this Playroma implementation. They are not scraped or copied from novels, games, websites, or third-party dialogue datasets.

User-imported corpora are expected to carry their own author/license/source metadata. Corpus Lab preserves imported source filename and SHA-256 information when available, but the user remains responsible for ensuring they have appropriate rights to train on and distribute derived content from a corpus.
